I am Xingjian Ran (冉行健). I am a Ph.D. student at [MMLab](https://mmlab.hk/), [The University of Hong Kong](https://www.hku.hk/) (HKU), under the supervision of Prof. [Bo Dai](https://daibo.info/). I received my B.Eng. from the [University of Electronic Science and Technology of China](https://www.uestc.edu.cn/) (UESTC). My research focuses on 3D/scene generation and spatial intelligence. I am always open to collaboration. Feel free to contact me if you are interested in working together!
